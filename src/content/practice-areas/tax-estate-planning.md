faqs:
  - question: Do I need a trust or is a will enough?
    answer: It depends on your estate size, family circumstances, and privacy preferences. A will goes through probate; a revocable living trust can avoid it. We help you choose the right structure for your goals.
  - question: What happens if I die without an estate plan in Oregon?
    answer: Your assets are distributed under Oregon's intestacy laws, which may not reflect your wishes, and the probate process can be lengthy and costly for your heirs.

    answer: Strategies include irrevocable trusts, annual gifting programs, charitable planning, and family limited partnerships. We tailor a plan based on your asset profile and family goals.

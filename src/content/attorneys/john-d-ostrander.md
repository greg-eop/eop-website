John Ostrander specializes in trial work, focusing on commercial, employment, insurance, tort and transportation litigation.

He is a graduate of Grinnell College, The London School of Economics, and Washington University Law School. He is a former judicial clerk to the Honorable William M. Dale, Jr., of the U.S. District Court for the District of Oregon.  John is AV® peer review rated by Martindale-Hubbell, and was named to the Oregon Super Lawyers® List for the past ten years in the area of Business Litigation.  John is a member of the Oregon and Washington State Bars.  John is also admitted to practice before the U.S. District Court for the District of Oregon, the Western District of Washington, the Eastern District of Washington, and the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals.

John actively participates in a number of court and community pro bono activities.  John and his family enjoy the outdoors, athletics, and travel.

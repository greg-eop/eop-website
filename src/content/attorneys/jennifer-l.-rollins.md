Jennifer Rollins practices in the areas of business, real estate and finance. She is a graduate of Wittenberg University (Springfield, Ohio) and the University of Virginia Law School. During law school, Ms. Rollins served as executive editor of the Virginia Environmental Law Journal.

She is a member of the Oregon State Bar and the Multnomah County Bar Association. She is also a volunteer for CASA, Court Appointed Special Advocates for Children.
